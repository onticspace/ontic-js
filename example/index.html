    How this works?

    1. Prove account ownership

      Users prove account ownership on Ontic Space.

    2. Anchor the record in time

      Proof hashes are committed to Ethereum, establishing when the records existed.

    3. Take the proof anywhere

      Any app can verify these dated proofs locally and on Ethereum, without calling Ontic Space.

    One contract read checks the committed hash; the SDK also verifies the network, block, and timestamp.
